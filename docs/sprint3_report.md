### Findings requiring Sprint 4 work

The recorded accuracy/FPR values are below a deployment-quality baseline and
are not presented as meeting the plan's ~95–97% target. Increase and validate
training coverage, investigate temporal/generalization behavior, tune models
on validation, then freeze/evaluate under the Sprint 4 protocol.

LinearSVC reported `Liblinear failed to converge, increase the number of
iterations.` The warning remains attached to the run and visible in the UI.
The MLP trained for ten epochs; checkpoint 9 had the lowest validation BCE
(about 369.31). This unusually high loss despite falling training loss needs
an explicit investigation of feature ranges, outliers and generalization in
Sprint 4. These scores must not be interpreted as calibrated file safety.
